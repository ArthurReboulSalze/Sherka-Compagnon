# Sherka 0.4.0

Sherka now welcomes you in French, English, Spanish, Italian, German, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.

- Seven interface languages across the control panel, widget and tray menu.
- A ready-to-use Sherka personality and instructions in each language. Choose an interface language and the original companion follows it automatically.
- Cleaner settings, aligned audio selections and sliders with live percentage values.
- Custom characters keep their own language, personality, models and voices.

Choose a conversation model in LM Studio and compatible speech models and voices in VoiceStudio. Your provider choices remain under your control. VoiceStudio is required; LM Studio is currently the only conversation provider.

Update an existing copy from **Settings → Updates**, or download the Windows x64 installer or no-install ZIP below. Profiles and memories stay in your Windows user folder.

Avatar animations, expressions and moods remain planned.

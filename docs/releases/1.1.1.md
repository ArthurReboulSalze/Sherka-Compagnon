# Sherka 1.1.1 — desktop controls within reach

Move from your companion to its buttons without watching them disappear. This update fixes transparent gaps that could hide the toolbar and resize handle, especially with Minerva enlarged to maximum size.

- Text, image and resize controls remain accessible while approaching them.
- Controls still hide when you leave the companion's area or switch focus.
- Overlapping conversation images remain behind the avatar and keep their own controls.

Five focused native Windows tests cover maximum-size Minerva, pointer resizing, hover/click/keyboard behavior, text/image cells and layouts restored after restarting. The fix was also tested by the user who reported it.

## Downloads and updates

- **Windows x64 installer** — install normally or check **Settings → Updates** in Sherka.
- **Windows x64 portable ZIP** — extract the complete archive and run `sherka-compagnon.exe`.
- **Debian x64 package** — install the `.deb`; Linux updates remain manual.

This is a maintenance update to [Sherka 1.1](1.1.0.md). Your companion settings, conversations and memories stay on your device.

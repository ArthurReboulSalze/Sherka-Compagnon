# Sherka 0.8.1 — Fresh faces, familiar personalities

**Alba joins your desktop with a white-and-gold android design**, replacing Candis's images and name while keeping her warm, playful personality. **Minerva gets a new armored look** and keeps her sharp tongue. Both include all seven expressions and the existing smooth half-second transitions.

- **A clearer icon:** the recropped golden logo fills more of the Windows, tray and installer icons, and appears throughout the interface.
- **Your companions stay yours:** the original Candis profile becomes Alba automatically, keeping its model, voice, conversations and memories. Edited names, personality text and custom images are preserved.
- **Updated presentation:** new portraits, descriptions and a fresh control-panel screenshot in the README.

## Choose your download

- **Installer:** `Sherka-Compagnon_0.8.1_x64-setup.exe`.
- **No installation:** extract `Sherka-Compagnon_0.8.1_windows-x64-portable.zip` and launch the executable inside.
- **Already using Sherka?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** to install this signed update.

Windows x64, WebView2 and the Visual C++ x64 runtime are required. VoiceStudio, models and voices are installed separately. See the [setup guide](https://github.com/ArthurReboulSalze/Sherka-Compagnon/blob/v0.8.1/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md).

The direct executable, update signatures, `latest.json` and checksums are also attached. Installed and no-install copies share your Windows user data; use 0.8.0 or later after upgrading from an older version.

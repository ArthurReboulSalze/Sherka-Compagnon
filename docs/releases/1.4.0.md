# Sherka 1.4.0 — three's company

- **Invite a second companion.** Press **Add a companion** in the widget toolbar and pick a guest. Your active companion stays the host and keeps the controls; the guest appears in its own transparent window with its seven expressions and its own voice. Move and resize it freely; its place is remembered.
- **One answer, then a real exchange.** Exactly one companion answers you: the one you name at the start ("Minerva, tell me a joke"), or one chosen at random. If you stay silent, the other one reacts after a delay, then the two keep the conversation going until you speak, type or press **Stop**.
- **Two voices, never at once.** The companions speak in turn, without interrupting each other. The next reply is prepared while the current one is spoken, so the exchange flows without pauses. Interrupting stops both, and what was heard is shared with the other companion.
- **They know who is talking.** Your lines and each companion's lines are labelled in what the models read, so neither mistakes you for the other companion. Add **Your first name** in Settings to be called by your name.
- **A warm welcome.** When a guest arrives, the host greets it with a short remark in character. If you say nothing, the guest replies after the delay and the conversation starts on its own.
- **One model for both.** The guest borrows the host's provider and model, so only one LM Studio model stays loaded. Each keeps its own personality, voice, memory and mood, and both keep learning about you from your own words.
- **Your pace.** In **Settings → Three-way conversations**, choose the silence delay (5, 8, 10 or 15 seconds, or off) and **Favor quick replies** for shorter turns. **Recent** pairs in the picker resume a previous three-way conversation.

Also in this release:

- At startup, the widget shows the Sherka logo and the VoiceStudio status until the voice service is ready, then your companion fades in. A slow VoiceStudio start is no longer reported as an error.
- Images during conversation start off when Sherka starts or when you switch companion, so ComfyUI only starts when you press **Image**.

Hermes companions, companions without a voice and conversation illustrations are not available in a three-way conversation. With a paid cloud provider, every companion reply is a billed request, including prepared reactions that are dropped when you speak.

## Downloads and setup

- **Windows x64 installer** — install normally or check **Settings → Updates** in Sherka.
- **Windows x64 portable ZIP** — extract the complete archive and run `sherka-compagnon.exe`.
- **Debian x64 package** — install the `.deb`; Linux updates remain manual.

Profiles, voices, conversations and memories stay in place. The database moves to schema 10 to record who said each line, so do not open your data with an earlier version afterwards. Automated checks passed on Windows and Linux, including the native Windows interface tests, and the author tested three-way conversations by voice in the Windows app. On Debian 13, the released package starts with isolated data under a virtual display and its widget shows the companion; physical Linux audio and desktop behavior still need broader testing. No personal conversations, saved memories or API keys are included in these downloads.

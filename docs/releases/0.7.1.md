# Sherka 0.7.1 — A new face for the app

Sherka gets its own application identity: a blue-haired AI with starry eyes, distinct from the companion you choose for your desktop.

- New Windows executable, tray, sidebar and favicon artwork.
- Reliable icon refresh when rebuilding the application.
- A refreshed English README with cyan accents and an updated control-panel screenshot.

Your companion portraits, voices, conversations and memories stay in place.

## Choose your download

- **Installer:** `Sherka-Compagnon_0.7.1_x64-setup.exe`.
- **No installation:** extract `Sherka-Compagnon_0.7.1_windows-x64-portable.zip`, then launch the executable inside.
- **Already using Sherka?** Open **Settings → Check for updates** to install this signed update.

Windows x64, WebView2 and the Visual C++ x64 runtime are required. VoiceStudio and your speech models are installed separately. See the [setup guide](https://github.com/ArthurReboulSalze/Sherka-Compagnon/blob/v0.7.1/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md).

The direct executable, update signatures, `latest.json` and checksums are also attached for updates and verification.
